Introduction

This document sets out the Cookies Policy of Virtuous Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 93 632 168 355) (referred to in this cookies policy as ‘we’, ‘us’, or ‘our’).

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time by posting an updated copy on our website and we encourage you to check our website regularly to ensure that you are aware of our most current Cookies Policy.

Types of cookies we use

A cookie is a small file that’s stored on your computer or device when you visit a website that uses cookies. Our Website uses Cloudflare for hosting and security. Cloudflare may set necessary security or traffic-management cookies depending on the features enabled. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies to profile users or track them across websites or apps.

The below table explains our approach to each type of cookie.

Strictly Necessary Cookies Cloudflare may set cookies to secure the Website, verify security challenges or manage traffic, depending on the Cloudflare features enabled. These cookies support delivery and protection of the Website rather than behavioural advertising.
Performance Cookies We do not use performance or analytics cookies to analyse visitors’ behaviour.
Functional Cookies We do not use cookies to personalise the Website based on your interests or browsing behaviour. Necessary security or traffic-management cookies used by Cloudflare are described in the Strictly Necessary Cookies row.
Advertising cookies We do not use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising trackers on the Website.
Content cookies The Website loads fonts from Google. This requires a request to Google’s servers and involves technical information handling, as explained in our Privacy Policy. We do not use content cookies for behavioural advertising.

How long will cookies remain on my device?

Cookie duration depends on the Cloudflare feature and settings in use. Some cookies last for a browsing session and others remain until expiry or deletion. Where enabled, Cloudflare’s __cf_bm bot-protection cookie expires after 30 minutes of continuous inactivity; challenge cookies last for the validity period configured for the challenge. You can delete cookies through your browser.

How do third parties use cookies on the website?

Cloudflare may set cookies needed to secure the Website, distinguish automated traffic or manage requests. The cookies used depend on our Cloudflare configuration. Our Website also loads Google Fonts: your browser contacts Google to obtain the font files and sends technical request information, including your IP address and browser information. Font delivery and its information handling are separate from analytics or advertising cookies. Our Privacy Policy explains third-party and overseas handling. If you follow a link to another website, that website may use its own cookies under its own policies.

How do i control cookies?

Usually, you can control and manage cookies through your browser. You can control whether or not your browser accepts cookies, how to filter and manage cookies, and how to delete cookies at the end of a session.

Blocking or removing necessary Cloudflare cookies may prevent a security challenge from completing or affect access to the Website. Blocking requests to Google’s font services may change how text is displayed.

If our use of cookies changes, we will update this policy to describe the cookies and their purposes, and seek consent where required.
