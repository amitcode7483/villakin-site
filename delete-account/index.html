Delete your Villakin account

Villakin is made by Virtuous Solutions Pty Ltd (the developer name on Google Play and the App Store). You can delete your family’s account and data at any time, yourself, in the app.

Delete your whole account, in the app

  1. Open Villakin and sign in.
  2. Go to Edit family details.
  3. Scroll to Delete account and tap Delete our family’s account.
  4. Confirm twice. It happens straight away and can’t be undone.

This deletes your family for every parent in it: parents’ names and emails, your children’s profiles and “What helps” notes, teen logins, trips, chat messages, plans, holiday calendar and Nearby details. Circles your family started pass to another family in them, or are deleted if empty.

Can’t open the app?

Email villakinapp@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Villakin account”. We’ll check it’s you, delete the account and confirm by email within 7 days.

Delete some data, and keep your account

  • A child: Kids → tap the child → remove. Their profile, notes and teen login are deleted.
  • A teen login: Kids → the teen → Login and rules → Turn off.
  • A message you wrote: tap Delete under it in the chat.
  • Nearby: turn off “Be findable” and your Nearby details are removed straight away.
  • Leave a circle: open the circle → Leave circle.
  • Anything else: email villakinapp@gmail.com and tell us what to delete.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Database backups: deleted data can stay in a backup for up to 7 days, then it’s overwritten.
  • Action log: a record of actions (like “joined a circle”), with no content, kept for 12 months. After you delete your account it holds only random IDs that no longer link to you.
  • Reports: a message someone reported may be kept until our staff have reviewed it.
  • Teen trip locations are already deleted as soon as each trip ends.
