Villakin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Villakin is a family app for Australian parents. It helps families you know plan playdates, swap care in the school holidays, find classes, and give teenagers a little independence with their parents in the loop.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and your choices. We've written it to be read by parents, and a short version for teens is near the end.

Villakin is run by Virtuous Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 93 632 168 355) ("Villakin", "we", "us"). Contact: villakinapp@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work. No ads, no selling data, no tracking across other apps or websites.

  • Other families see you only if you're in the same invite-only circle, and then only your first name, family name and suburb, and your children's first names, age range and interests.

  • A home address is shown only to families who said yes to a plan there, and only until the plan ends.

  • A teen's location is shared only during a trip their parent approved, only with their own parents, and is deleted as soon as the trip ends. Before a teen's first trip, the teen and a parent each agree to how this works.

  • Your data is stored in Australia (Amazon Web Services, Sydney).

  • You can delete your family's account and data at any time in the app.

Collected automatically

  • Basic server logs (which screen of the API was called, when, and whether it worked), kept by our hosting provider for troubleshooting.

We don't use analytics or advertising tools, and we don't use cookies.

4. How long we keep it

In short:

  • Your account data is kept while your family has an account. When you delete your account, it's deleted, including your children's details, notes, teen logins, trips, messages and calendar.

  • Teen trip locations: deleted as soon as the trip ends. If nobody ends it, deleted a day after the planned home time.

  • Home addresses on plans: removed 7 days after the plan ends.
  • Sign-in codes: deleted a day after they're used or expire.
  • Notifications in the app: 90 days. Past holiday calendar days: 30 days.
  • The action log (no content): 12 months.
  • Database backups: up to 7 days, then overwritten.

5. Keeping it safe

  • Data is stored in Australia, in a private database that isn't reachable from the internet, and travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).

  • Passwords are stored only as strong one-way hashes (Argon2). Sign-in codes are stored only as keyed hashes.

  • Logins on a phone are kept in the phone's secure keychain.
  • You can sign out every other device at once, and resetting your password does this automatically.
  • Staff tools need a second step (an emailed code), and staff see only what they need.
  • We'll tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, as the law requires. We have a written plan for this.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix problems quickly.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your details: in the app (Edit family details, each child's page), or email us.
  • Get a copy of your data: email villakinapp@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Delete your account: Edit family details → Delete our family's account. It's immediate and permanent.
  • Turn off a teen's login: on your teen's page. It stops working straight away.
  • Notifications: choose what you get, and set quiet hours, in Notifications.
  • Complain: email us first and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our answer, you can contact the OAIC at oaic.gov.au or 1300 363 992.

7. Children and teens

Villakin is designed for parents. Children under 11 don't have their own login; their parent manages everything. Teens aged 11–17 can have a login only if their parent sets it up, and the parent stays in the loop through approval rules and trip check-ins.

For teens, in short

Your parent set up your Villakin login. Your friends in your circles can see your first name and the activities you post. Your location is only shared during a trip you've started, only with your own parents, and it's deleted as soon as the trip ends. Nobody at Villakin reads your stuff. If you're ever in danger, call 000.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
