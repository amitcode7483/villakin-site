Villakin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Introduction

This document sets out the privacy policy of Virtuous Solutions Pty Ltd (ABN 93 632 168 355), the operator of Villakin (referred to in this privacy policy as ‘we’, ‘us’, or ‘our’). It covers personal information handled through the Villakin app, our website at https://villakin.com.au, and enquiries to us.

We take our privacy obligations seriously and we’ve created this privacy policy to explain how we store, maintain, use and disclose personal information.

We are committed to preventing serious invasions of privacy and ensuring the protection of your personal information, so you can contact us using the details below if you have any questions or concerns.

This privacy policy explains our information handling practices. Where consent is required for a particular collection, use or disclosure, we seek that consent separately; providing information does not give blanket consent to unrelated uses.

We may change this privacy policy from time to time by posting an updated copy on our website. We will notify users through the app before a material change takes effect, and we encourage you to check our website regularly to ensure that you are aware of our most current privacy policy.

Types of personal information we collect

The personal information we collect may include the following:

  • parents’ names and family names;

  • suburb and, for a plan at someone’s home, the street address for that plan;

  • parents’ email addresses;

  • account credentials, including password hashes, teen usernames and sign-in, verification, invitation and setup codes;

  • account identifiers and email information supplied by Apple or Google if you choose those sign-in methods;

  • children’s first names, age ranges and interests, entered by their parent or guardian, and optional “What helps” notes;

  • group and circle memberships and invitation information;

  • messages in circle chats and plan threads, Nearby requests, hellos and chats, and reports and blocks;

  • account and notification settings;

  • device notification tokens, where notifications are enabled;

  • a teen’s location during a parent-approved trip, including location at trip check-ins or an SOS alert, records of trip-location consent, an action log without message content, and technical information used for security and troubleshooting;

  • information you provide when contacting us for support or making a complaint; and

  • holiday calendar entries, plans, activities, teen trip details, class listings and reports, and optional Nearby profile and open-circle listing information.

We collect only personal information reasonably necessary to operate Villakin and respond to enquiries. We do not ask for children’s surnames, dates of birth, photographs, school details or contact details. A suburb is used for family profiles and Nearby discovery; a home address is collected only for a plan held at that home.

Please avoid including unnecessary personal information in messages, descriptions or hello lines. Circle chats and plan threads allow families to communicate about their arrangements. Short Nearby chats allow messages of up to 1,000 characters.

Children’s personal information

Villakin is designed for parents and guardians, with parent-managed child profiles and optional logins for teens aged 11–17. Parents enter a child’s first name, age range and interests. Children under 11 do not have their own login. Teens may post and join activities and plan trips subject to the rules and approvals set by their parent.

A parent sets up a teen’s username and password using a setup code. Teens can see their own activities, trips and the rules their parent has set; they cannot see or post in parent chats or see prices or payments.

Parents or guardians may contact us at villakinapp@gmail.com to review, manage, or request deletion of their child’s personal information.

Sensitive information

Optional “What helps” notes help families make suitable plans and may reveal health or other sensitive information, including disability or support needs, even without a diagnosis. Such information may also appear in messages, plan notes, reports or enquiries. Please include only necessary details and avoid diagnoses or unnecessary medical information.

We collect sensitive information only where reasonably necessary and with express consent, unless a legal exception applies. We explain the purpose and recipients and seek consent separately from acceptance of this policy. For children, we consider their capacity to understand the proposed handling and obtain their consent if capable, or an authorised parent’s or guardian’s consent if not. Account setup alone is not consent.

Parents in the child’s family account can see “What helps” notes; families attending the same plan can see them until it ends. Notes are excluded from Nearby listings and push notifications. Staff may see sensitive information in reported content or information sent to support. Other use or disclosure requires consent or a legal basis.

Remove a note in the app or contact us to withdraw consent or request deletion. We stop relying on withdrawn consent and take reasonable steps to remove controlled active records and plan copies, subject to legal retention obligations. Deleted information may remain in backups for up to 7 days; deletion is reapplied after restoration. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful handling or recall copies held by other families.

If we receive sensitive information we have not requested, we assess whether we could lawfully have collected it. If not, we destroy or de-identify it as soon as practicable where lawful and reasonable. Our other retention and security practices also apply.

How we collect personal information

We may collect personal information either directly from you, or from other members of your family or parent-controlled group, including where you:

  • contact us through our website;

  • create or use a Villakin account or group;

  • submit an online sign-up form;

  • communicate with us via email or otherwise;

  • share information in a circle, plan or Nearby interaction, or use a parent-approved teen trip; and

  • enable notifications or choose a third-party sign-in method.

We do not use analytics tools or advertising trackers in Villakin or on our website. Limited technical records are used for security and troubleshooting, rather than advertising or tracking users across websites or apps. Our website uses Cloudflare for hosting and security and loads fonts from Google. These providers receive technical information, such as your IP address and browser or request information. Cloudflare may set necessary security or traffic-management cookies depending on the features enabled. Our Cookies Policy explains our website cookie practices.

Use of your personal information

We collect and use personal information for the following purposes:

  • to provide Villakin and information you request;

  • for account administration, security and troubleshooting;

  • to make information available to the family members, circle members, plan participants or Nearby recipients described in this policy, and to service providers only as needed to operate Villakin;

  • to respond to support enquiries, correct faults, moderate reported content and prevent misuse;

  • to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce our agreements with third parties;

  • to send you administrative messages, reminders, notices, updates, security alerts, and other information requested by you; and

  • to manage requests to access, correct or delete personal information and respond to privacy complaints.

The Villakin app’s server and database are hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Sydney, Australia. AWS also provides our app email service. Australian app hosting does not mean all personal information is handled only in Australia: the overseas services described here also receive information needed to provide their functions.

Expo (650 Industries) in the United States and Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, operating in the United States and other countries, deliver push notifications. They receive device notification tokens and notification content needed for delivery. Notifications may contain names or plan information, but do not contain teen locations or “What helps” notes.

If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, those providers handle authentication information and supply the account identifier and email information used to sign you in. Our website uses Cloudflare for hosting and security and Google Fonts for font delivery. Those services receive technical request information, including IP addresses and browser information. They may process information in the United States and other countries where their services operate. You may contact us for further information about likely recipient countries.

Where we disclose personal information to an overseas recipient, we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, unless an applicable legal exception applies. Use of Villakin or a third-party sign-in option does not by itself waive those protections. We do not sell or rent personal information or share it with advertisers. We may disclose information where required or authorised by law, including where necessary and legally permitted to respond to a serious threat to life, health or safety.

Who can see your information

Families in your circles can see your first name, family name and suburb, your children’s first names, age ranges and interests, holiday calendar entries, plans in that circle and circle chat messages. Joining a circle makes that circle information available to its members. Circle owners control admission and membership; circles may be joined by invitation or an accepted Nearby request.

Families attending a plan at your home can see the street address for that plan until the plan ends. The address is then removed from the plan record 7 days after the plan ends. A child’s “What helps” note is shared only with families attending the same plan as that child, until the plan ends.

Another parent or partner who joins your family using the family code can see and manage everything in that family account, including children’s details, circles, plans, messages, teen trips and location during a trip. Each parent has a separate login. The parent who started the family can remove another parent. Take care who you invite to share your family account.

Teen trip location

Location is collected only from a teen during a trip approved by their parent and started by the teen, including at trip check-ins or an SOS alert. It is visible only to that teen’s own parents, including another parent who has joined the family account. It is not shared with other circle members or Nearby families and is not used for advertising or analytics.

Before the first trip, the teen and a parent each read and agree in the app to how trip-location sharing works; we record the dates of those agreements. Collection also depends on the phone’s location permission. A teen can withdraw device location permission, and a parent can disable the teen’s login. Disabling location permission prevents further location collection and affects trip-location functionality.

Trip coordinates, including pings and positions recorded at check-ins or SOS, are deleted from the live app when the trip ends, including when marked Home safe, ended by a parent, cancelled or declined. If the trip is not ended, coordinates are deleted 24 hours after the planned home time. The remaining trip log retains times only, not coordinates.

Staff access and reports

Staff can access account details needed for support, including names, email addresses, family names, suburbs and email-confirmation status, and counts and the action log, which does not contain message content. Staff can read a chat message, join request or hello only when a user reports it, for moderation. We do not routinely read or monitor unreported chats. Information you separately send us with a support enquiry or complaint is also available to staff handling it. We may access or disclose information where required or authorised by law.

Nearby families and open circles

Nearby is an optional feature when available. “Be findable” is off by default. Each parent chooses whether to enable it. If enabled, families in the same or nearby suburbs can see only that parent’s first name, family name, suburb and short hello line. The listing does not show children’s names, ages, photographs or schools, a street address or a map location.

A circle owner may choose “Open in my area”. Nearby families can then see the circle’s name, kind, suburb, number of families and short description, and can ask to join. The listing does not reveal members’ identities, children’s details, plans or circle chat. Those circle details become available only after the owner accepts the family’s request to join.

A family may send a join request to an open circle or a hello to a findable parent. The recipient sees the sender’s first name, family name, suburb and short request or hello message. Ongoing Nearby chat starts only after the recipient accepts; circle chat and membership require acceptance by the circle owner. Nearby therefore enables contact with families outside your existing circles, but does not provide unrestricted messaging to strangers.

Nearby requires a confirmed email address and applies daily request limits. Parents can report or block a family, and staff can review reported messages, requests or hellos. A parent can turn off “Be findable”, and a circle owner can make an open circle private, at any time. Turning off discoverability removes the relevant Nearby listing; it does not itself erase earlier requests, accepted chats or existing circle memberships, which have the retention periods described in this policy.

Security

We take reasonable steps to ensure your personal information is secure and protected from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. We use password protection, access controls and other administrative and technical measures. App data travels using HTTPS/TLS, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and staff tools require a password and a one-time emailed code. These safeguards and restrictions on staff access are not end-to-end encryption. However, we cannot guarantee the security of your personal information.

Retention and deletion

We retain personal information for the periods described here, subject to any legal requirement to retain it. We take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify personal information when it is no longer needed. In particular:

  • Family and account information, child profiles, interests and “What helps” notes: while the family has an account, unless removed earlier by a parent. A teen’s login is deleted when the parent disables it or deletes the family account;

  • circles, memberships, plans, activities and circle or plan messages: while the family has an account and the relevant circle exists. On account deletion, a circle started by that family may pass to another family, or be removed if empty;

  • teen trip coordinates: until the trip ends or, if it is not ended, 24 hours after the planned home time. The trip log retains times only;

  • home addresses on plans: removed 7 days after the plan ends;

  • holiday calendar days: 30 days after the relevant day; in-app notifications: 90 days;

  • Nearby discoverability details: shown until the parent turns off “Be findable” or deletes the account. Open-circle listings: shown until the owner makes the circle private;

  • answered Nearby join requests and hellos, with their associated short chats: 90 days after the answer, except accepted hellos and their chats, which are kept until either family deletes its account. Reported requests or hellos are not deleted under the 90-day rule while awaiting staff review;

  • Nearby blocks between families: until either family deletes its account;

  • sign-in, email-confirmation, password-reset and staff codes: 1 day after use or expiry. Teen setup codes and circle invitation codes: 30 days after expiry, use or cancellation;

  • action logs, recording who did what and when without content: 12 months. Entries about a deleted family retain only random identifiers that no longer link to that family;

  • reported chat messages: until removed by staff or deleted with the relevant circle or account;

  • push notification tokens: until sign-out or the notification provider reports that the device is no longer available;

  • IP-address rate-limit counters: minutes in memory; staff alert counters: up to an hour in memory. Hosting-provider server logs are retained under the provider’s applicable limits for security and troubleshooting; and

  • database backups: a rolling period of up to 7 days.

You can delete your family’s account in the app using “Edit family details” then “Delete our family’s account”, or contact us for assistance. Account deletion removes your family’s data from the live app immediately, including child details, notes, teen logins, plans, trips, messages and calendar entries. Deleted information may remain in rolling database backups for up to 7 days before they are overwritten. We do not use those copies for ordinary operations, and if a backup is restored we reapply the deletion.

Turning off “Be findable” removes discoverability rather than deleting the underlying account details. Accounts are not currently deleted automatically solely because they are inactive. We retain support and complaint records only while reasonably needed to handle them and meet applicable legal obligations.
