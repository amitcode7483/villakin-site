How it works

Everything a family's village needs

From weekend playdates to the school holidays, with the families you already know.

Your circle, your villageYour class, your street, your team: the families you already know.
1

Circles you trust

Invite-only groups for your class, street or team. Only families in your circle see your kids.

2

Playdates and care swaps

Plan time together, or swap care days: "I'll take them Tuesday, you do Thursday".

3

School holidays, sorted

Mark the days you need care, can host or are away, and see who can help.

4

Classes and programs

Find holiday programs and term classes, and go together with a friend from your circle.

5

Teens, with you in the loop

Teens plan activities and trips; you approve. Live location only during a trip, and SOS if they need you.

6

Every kid belongs

Sensory-friendly, small group and step-free options, and private "what helps" notes in your own words.

Privacy

Private by design

Built with children's safety first, under Australian privacy law.

Only your circles

Other families see first names, family name, suburb and kids' age ranges, and only inside your circles.

Addresses stay private

A home address is shown only to families going to a plan there, and only until it ends.

Location only on trips

Teen location is shared only during a trip their parent approved, and deleted a week later.

Stored in Australia

Your data is kept in Sydney. No ads, and we never sell your information.

Yours to delete

Delete your family's account and data at any time, right in the app.
